Privacy Policy

The short version: TilePilot collects nothing. Everything it does happens on your Mac and stays there.

No data collection

TilePilot has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no accounts, and no tracking of any kind. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data — because the app never sends any.

Everything is local

Your snapping preferences and saved workspaces (app names, window positions, display layouts) are stored only on your Mac, in standard macOS preference and Application Support locations. They never leave your machine.

Permissions, explained

Accessibility lets TilePilot move and resize your windows — that is its entire job; it reads window positions and titles solely to snap and restore them. Automation (Apple Events) is used only at the moment you press Capture, to ask supported browsers which tabs are open so a workspace can reopen them. Neither permission is used for anything else.

Questions? Email iantrimble@outlook.com. If this policy ever changes, the change will be listed on this page and in the app's changelog before it takes effect.
