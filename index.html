Snap windows by dropping them where you mean.

What it does

Area-based snapping

Drop a window anywhere in a screen quadrant and it snaps there. Edge and corner targets still work; ⌘-drag moves freely. Multi-monitor drags land on the display you meant — never the neighbor.

Workspace capture & restore

Snapshot your running apps, window frames, and display arrangement. Restore the whole layout on demand — built for dock-and-undock laptop life.

Bring your muscle memory

Coming from Windows? ⌘-arrow snapping and quarter cycling. Mac loyalist? Rectangle-style shortcuts. Linux power user? Modal resize/move shortcuts, with Vim-style focus on the roadmap.
