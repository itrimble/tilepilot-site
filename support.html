Frequently asked

Windows aren't snapping

TilePilot needs the Accessibility permission to move windows: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → enable TilePilot, then relaunch the app.

macOS fights my drags

macOS has its own drag-to-edge tiling. For the best experience turn off System Settings → Desktop & Dock → “Drag windows to screen edges to tile.” TilePilot detects this and offers the shortcut; the green-button tiling menu keeps working.

How do I place a window without snapping?

Hold ⌘ while dragging, or drop along the middle cross of the screen. Snapping only arms when a window is decisively inside a quadrant, edge, or corner region.

A workspace didn't fully restore

Apps that were quit are relaunched when possible; windows on other Spaces can't be enumerated by macOS and are skipped. Capture again after arranging to refresh a stale snapshot.

Keyboard shortcuts

Three presets — Keep Mac feel, Coming from Windows, Linux Power User — in TilePilot Settings → General, with every shortcut rebindable under Settings → Shortcuts.
