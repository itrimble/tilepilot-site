What's new

Release history for TilePilot.

1.0.0 — July 2026

Initial Mac App Store release.

  • Area-based drag snapping: quadrant regions, radial corners, edge halves and maximize, with a free center cross and ⌘-drag override.
  • Multi-monitor display affinity: seam-overshoot grace, deliberate cross-display hand-off, decisive-flick transfer.
  • Workspace capture & restore with live previews and display-arrangement awareness.
  • Shortcut presets: Keep Mac feel, Coming from Windows, Linux Power User — all rebindable.
  • macOS-native-tiling coexistence: detection, guidance, and one-click deep link to the setting.
  • Dark/Light/Auto theming with refined SF typography; sub-300ms motion honoring Reduce Motion.
